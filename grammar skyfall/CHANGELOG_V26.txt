GRAMMAR SKYFALL V26 — SERIOUS MUSIC CONTROL

Music control was rebuilt instead of using a visual-only status label.

1. Added a polished dark/glass MUSIC ON / MUSIC OFF control fixed on the upper-right.
2. The control now actually starts/stops the HTMLAudioElement used for background music.
3. Music preference is saved in localStorage and survives page reloads.
4. Turning MUSIC OFF does NOT mute sound effects.
5. Turning MUSIC ON retries the current mode's music immediately.
6. Solo Quest keeps SOLO music; DEL PINO CHALLENGE keeps DEL PINO music.
7. The control is available across the app after the game has started, including Solo Quest.
8. The control is keyboard/touch friendly and exposes an accessible pressed state.
