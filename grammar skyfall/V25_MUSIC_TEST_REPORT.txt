V25 MUSIC TEST REPORT

Checked statically:
1. MUSIC toggle is mounted outside the game screen, so it remains usable on home, online, local test, Solo Quest and DEL PINO CHALLENGE screens.
2. Toggle stores its state in localStorage key psrMusicEnabled.
3. OFF calls stopMusic() and prevents future background playback.
4. ON resumes the last requested music track.
5. Sound effects no longer depend on the music flag, so turning MUSIC OFF does not disable roulette/correct/wrong/countdown SFX.
6. Existing music file slots remain: menu.mp3, gameplay.mp3, skyfall.mp3, victory.mp3, solo.mp3, delpino.mp3.
