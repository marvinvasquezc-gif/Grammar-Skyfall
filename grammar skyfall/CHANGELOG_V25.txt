GRAMMAR SKYFALL V25 — MUSIC TOGGLE HOTFIX

- Added persistent MUSIC ON/OFF control visible across the app, including Solo Quest and DEL PINO CHALLENGE.
- Turning music off stops background tracks immediately.
- Turning music back on resumes the current requested mode track.
- Music preference persists in localStorage across reloads.
- Music OFF does NOT mute sound effects; roulette, correct/wrong, countdown and other SFX still play.
- Existing menu/gameplay/skyfall/victory/solo/delpino tracks are preserved.
