GRAMMAR SKYFALL V22 — NEW ROUND ELIMINATION RULE

After every completed round with 3–6 survivors:

1. Find the survivor(s) on the lowest board tile.
2. If exactly one survivor is furthest back, that player is eliminated immediately.
3. If 2–6 survivors are tied on the same lowest tile, ONLY those tied players enter the tiebreaker.
4. Each tied player spins the 1–6 elimination roulette once.
5. Each tied player gets one random Present Simple question.
6. Wrong answer OR no answer before the timer = eliminated.
7. Among the tied players who answered correctly, ONLY the lowest roulette result may be eliminated. Everyone else survives, even when they did not roll the highest number.
8. If several correct players share the same lowest roulette result, nobody is eliminated by the roulette tie (wrong/timeout players are still eliminated).
9. If the round eliminates everyone except one survivor, that survivor wins immediately.
10. If exactly two survivors remain after the elimination phase, the existing two-player Muerte Súbita/Skyfall flow continues.

Online safety:
- Only the player's own device can spin or answer for that player.
- The host is authoritative for roulette results and answer validation.
- Question answers are not included in the public state sent to other clients.
